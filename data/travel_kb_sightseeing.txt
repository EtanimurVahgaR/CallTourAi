CallTourAI Travel KB — Sightseeing (Dubai)

Use this as a demo knowledge base for RAG queries about activities and ticketing.

ATTRACTIONS (demo)
- Burj Khalifa At The Top (Levels 124/125)
  Best time: sunset slots sell out first
  Ticket tip: book timed entry 2–5 days ahead in peak weeks
  Typical duration: 1.5–2 hours

- Dubai Fountain show (Downtown)
  Cost: free viewing areas
  Frequency: multiple evening shows
  Tip: arrive 20–30 minutes early for front spots

- Dubai Marina Dhow Cruise (dinner)
  Duration: ~2 hours cruise + boarding time
  Includes: buffet dinner
  Best for: first evening, low-effort family activity

- Desert safari (afternoon/evening)
  Includes: dune bashing + camp dinner
  Family note: confirm child ages; some operators limit very young kids for dune bashing

- Museum of the Future
  Good for: quick daytime visit
  Tip: timed tickets; mornings are less crowded

- Atlantis/Aquaventure-style waterpark day
  Best for: kids + full-day activity
  Tip: bring swimwear, sandals, and sunscreen

SCHEDULING RULES (demo)
- Do not stack: desert safari + late-night fountain + early morning Burj Khalifa next day.
- If user only has 4 nights: prioritize Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall, one desert safari, one theme/waterpark day, one cruise.
