CallTourAI Travel KB — Car Rentals (Dubai)

Use this as a demo knowledge base for RAG queries about rental cars and policies.

RENTAL OPTIONS (example inventory)
- Category: Economy
  Example models: Toyota Yaris (or similar)
  Seats/bags: 4 seats / 2 bags
  Typical price: 120–170 AED/day
  Deposit: 1500 AED

- Category: Compact SUV
  Example models: Nissan Kicks (or similar)
  Seats/bags: 5 seats / 3 bags
  Typical price: 180–260 AED/day
  Deposit: 2000 AED

- Category: Family SUV
  Example models: Toyota Fortuner (or similar)
  Seats/bags: 7 seats / 4 bags
  Typical price: 320–450 AED/day
  Deposit: 3000 AED

REQUIREMENTS (demo)
- Driver age: 21+ for Economy/Compact; 25+ for Family SUV.
- Documents: passport + valid driver’s license.
- International visitors: International Driving Permit (IDP) may be required depending on issuing country.

INSURANCE (demo)
- Standard CDW: included (higher excess)
- Full cover: +45 AED/day, lowers excess and includes basic glass/tyre coverage

FUEL + TOLLS (demo)
- Fuel policy: full-to-full
- Salik (toll): charged at cost + small admin fee per day

PICKUP/DROPOFF (demo)
- Airport pickup: available with 60 AED fee
- One-way dropoff: available within Dubai with 80 AED fee

BOOKING WORKFLOW (demo)
- To quote accurately, confirm: pickup date/time, dropoff date/time, passenger count, luggage, and whether child seats are needed.
- If user says "with kids" and needs space, recommend Compact SUV or Family SUV.
