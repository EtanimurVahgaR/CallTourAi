HOTEL CATALOG (example inventory)
- Hotel: Marina Family Suites
  Area: Dubai Marina
  Best for: families who want walkable dining + skyline views
  Room types: 1BR suite, 2BR suite
  Breakfast: included in Family Saver rate
  Pool: yes
  Kids club: yes (ages 4–12)
  Check-in/out: 15:00 / 12:00
  Cancellation: free until 48h before check-in; then 1-night penalty
  Notes: 5 min walk to tram; stroller-friendly

- Hotel: Downtown View Hotel
  Area: Downtown Dubai
  Best for: Burj Khalifa / Dubai Mall access
  Room types: deluxe king, family room (2 adults + 2 kids)
  Breakfast: optional add-on
  Pool: yes
  Kids club: no
  Cancellation: free until 72h before check-in; then non-refundable first night
  Notes: ideal for fountain show evenings

- Hotel: Palm Beach Resort
  Area: Palm Jumeirah
  Best for: beach + waterpark-style leisure
  Room types: resort room, family suite
  Breakfast: included
  Pool: yes (multiple)
  Kids club: yes
  Cancellation: free until 7 days before check-in; then 50% of stay
  Notes: beach cabanas available; requires taxi/ride-hail for most city sights

- Hotel: Old Dubai Boutique Inn
  Area: Al Seef / Bur Dubai
  Best for: cultural/heritage vibe, budget-conscious couples
  Room types: standard, superior
  Breakfast: included (light)
  Pool: no
  Cancellation: free until 24h before check-in
  Notes: close to souks and dhow cruise piers

RATE GUIDANCE (rough demo pricing)
- Mid-February family-friendly 4-star: typically 550–950 AED/night depending on area and view.
- Weekends (Fri/Sat) are usually higher priced.

BOOKING RULES (demo)
- When user says "book", always confirm: dates, adults/children ages, bed preference, and budget range.
- If the user requests "close to Burj Khalifa", prefer Downtown View Hotel.
- If the user requests "beach + kids club", prefer Palm Beach Resort.
- If the user requests "walkable nightlife and marina", prefer Marina Family Suites.
