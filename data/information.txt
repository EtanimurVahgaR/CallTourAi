For a family trip to Dubai next week (mid-February 2026), the weather is ideal, with pleasant daytime temperatures and cool evenings around 15°C. 
4-Night Family Itinerary
Day 1: Arrival & Dhow Cruise
Morning: Arrive at DXB and use your pre-booked private transfer to reach your hotel.
Evening: Enjoy a Marina Dhow Cruise with an international buffet dinner and views of the glittering skyline.
Day 2: Iconic Landmarks & Fountains
Day: Visit the Burj Khalifa (124th floor) and explore The Dubai Mall, which features an aquarium and ice rink.
Evening: Watch the free Dubai Fountain show, followed by dinner at a nearby restaurant with a view.
Day 3: Desert Adventure (Activity 1)
Afternoon/Evening: Desert Safari including dune bashing, camel rides, sandboarding, and a BBQ dinner with cultural performances.
Day 4: Theme Park Fun (Activity 2)
Full Day: Choose a major theme park based on age; Legoland Dubai is perfect for younger kids, while IMG Worlds of Adventure (indoor) or Aquaventure Waterpark at Atlantis are hits for all ages.
Day 5: Departure
Morning: Visit the Museum of the Future or enjoy a final walk at Kite Beach for views of the Burj Al Arab before your airport transfer. /
